Prepaid Wallet Architecture

How the MsgOrbit Prepaid Wallet Model Works

Enjoy absolute financial transparency with pre-launch cost estimation, temporary fund holds, and instant automated refunds for undelivered messages.

01

Prepaid Wallet Top-Ups

Maintain an active organization credit balance with zero hidden maintenance fees and zero platform markups on message delivery.

02

Pre-Launch Estimation

Before any broadcast launches, MsgOrbit calculates the exact estimated cost, automatically deducting opted-out suppression contacts.

03

Temporary Reserve Hold

The estimated campaign amount is temporarily held in reserve to prevent accidental overdrafts while messages dispatch through Meta.

04

Automated Instant Refunds

Costs settle strictly for confirmed delivered messages. Unused funds from failed, blocked, or invalid numbers refund to your wallet immediately.

Pricing FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about MsgOrbit SaaS plans, WhatsApp delivery rates, prepaid wallet billing, and refund guarantees.

MsgOrbit operates on a 100% transparent prepaid wallet model. You maintain an active wallet balance for message delivery. When you schedule or broadcast a campaign, our system estimates the required amount, temporarily holds it during dispatch, and finalizes charges strictly for delivered messages. You can top up your organization wallet balance anytime with zero maintenance fees.
If any message in your campaign cannot be delivered (due to invalid phone numbers, network timeouts, or recipient blocks), Meta's delivery webhook triggers an immediate settlement. The reserved amount for each undelivered message is automatically and instantly refunded back to your active wallet balance with a clear audit ledger entry.

MsgOrbit never adds hidden platform markups to these delivery rates.
