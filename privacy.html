🔒 Data Privacy & Protection

Privacy Policy

At MsgOrbit, we take your data security and privacy seriously. This policy outlines how we collect, use, and protect your information across our multi-tenant SaaS platform.

Section 1

Information We Collect

Organization & Account Details

Information provided during registration and profile management (Name, business email, phone number, address, website, GST number, and billing currency/timezone).

WhatsApp Business Data

Connected WhatsApp Business Account (WABA) metadata, phone numbers, verified display names, and approved message templates synced via Meta's APIs.

Contact Lists & Campaign Data

Customer contact information uploaded via CSV/Excel imports, custom tags, contact groups, and message broadcast history.

Usage & Device Metadata

Session metadata captured on every login (IP address, device type, browser, operating system) and immutable audit logs recording user actions.

Section 2

How We Use Your Information

Platform Delivery

To operate and maintain the multi-tenant SaaS dashboard, including campaign scheduling, bot execution, and conversation inbox synchronization.

Meta & API Compliance

To submit message templates for approval, process WhatsApp Flows, and enforce suppression lists (such as STOP keyword opt-outs).

Security & Authentication

To manage secure token rotation (JWT), hash passwords using bcrypt, and prevent unauthorized access or account enumeration.

Financial Settlements

To calculate pre-campaign estimated costs, reserve wallet funds, and process transaction histories.

Section 3

Tenant Isolation & Data Security

Strict Logical Separation

MsgOrbit implements robust tenant isolation architecture ensuring organizations can never access, view, or modify each other's data, contacts, or campaigns.

Encryption & Redaction

All sensitive fields (tokens, passwords, OTP codes hashed with HMAC-SHA256) are securely encrypted. Sensitive parameters are automatically redacted from system logs.

Section 4

User Rights & Contact

Data Access & Management

Organizations can view, edit, or delete their profile data, contact directories, and campaign settings at any time through their user panel.

Privacy Inquiries

For questions regarding this policy or data practices, contact our compliance team at privacy@msgorbit.com.
