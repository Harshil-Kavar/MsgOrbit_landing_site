Module 1

Precision Audience Targeting & Contact Management

Maintain isolated, organized contact databases and segment your audience for maximum campaign impact.

Individual Profiles

Add, edit, and delete contacts with rich attributes including first name, last name, phone, email, company, job title, date of birth, and address. View full details instantly via a convenient side drawer.

Bulk CSV/Excel Import

Upload large contact lists effortlessly using downloadable import templates, complete with success and failure status reporting.

Custom Tags & Groups

Categorize contacts with tags (e.g., "VIP", "Lead", "Customer") and build named groups (e.g., "Mumbai Customers") to use as precise audience targeting layers for your campaigns.

Module 2

Native Meta Integration & Interactive Flows

Connect your official WhatsApp Business Accounts (WABA) and deploy rich, interactive messaging experiences.

WABA Embedded Signup

Seamlessly connect and verify your WhatsApp Business Account via Meta's official flow. Monitor connection health (Connected, Disconnected, Error) and sync business profile metadata.

Approved Message Templates

Create Marketing, Utility, and Authentication templates supporting rich headers (Text, Image, Video, Document, Location), body copy, footers, and interactive buttons (Quick Reply, URL, Phone, Code, Flow) with live chat bubble previews.

Interactive WhatsApp Flows

Upload custom JSON definitions to deploy structured conversation forms directly inside chats and track deployment states.

Module 3

Smart Campaigns & Automated Compliance

Launch high-volume broadcasts with absolute control over dispatch rates, scheduling, and compliance.

Step-by-Step Campaign Wizard

Choose your sender number, select templates, target specific groups/tags, map dynamic variables with custom fallbacks, and schedule launches or send immediately.

Validation & Financial Preview

Pre-check eligible recipient counts, skip reasons, and estimated message costs before hitting launch.

Real-Time Delivery Funnel

Track messages live across Total → Eligible → Sent → Delivered → Read → Failed stages, alongside detailed error codes.

Marketing Suppression List

Automatically skip suppressed phone numbers, user opt-outs, and inbound STOP keyword requests to ensure total regulatory compliance.

Module 4

24/7 Conversational Automation

Build complex, engaging customer journeys without writing a single line of code.

Node-Based Visual Builder

Drag and drop nodes (Send Message, Wait for Input, Condition Branch, Terminal) to design conversation workflows triggered by keywords or message types.

Live Bot Simulator

Test your bot logic using a built-in simulator that mimics inbound media types, text, and contact states before going live.

Versioning & Execution History

Review bot version history, revert changes instantly, and inspect detailed logs of every execution path taken by contacts.

Module 5

Collaborative Support & Transparent Wallet Tracking

Keep your support team synchronized and manage your marketing budget with complete financial clarity.

Unified Conversation Inbox

View all inbound and outbound chats organized by contact with status filters (Open, Closed, Archived), chat-style timelines, and instant tag management from the contact detail pane.

Organization Wallet & Transactions

Monitor wallet balances in real-time, view detailed debit/credit logs with transaction references, and track automated cost reservations and refunds for campaigns.
