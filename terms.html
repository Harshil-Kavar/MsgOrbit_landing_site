Section 1

Acceptance of Terms & Platform Access

Service Scope

MsgOrbit provides a multi-tenant WhatsApp marketing, automation, and conversational inbox SaaS solution.

Account Eligibility

Users must represent a legitimate business entity and maintain active, verified WhatsApp Business Accounts (WABA) compliant with Meta's Commerce and Business Policies.

Organization Deactivation

System administrators reserve the right to suspend or deactivate organizations and associated user accounts that violate platform policies or terms of service.

Section 2

Prepaid Wallet & Financial Terms

Wallet Balance Model

Platform services operate on a prepaid wallet balance. Users must maintain sufficient funds to cover broadcast campaigns.

Cost Estimation & Settlement

Estimated message costs are calculated and reserved prior to campaign launch. Actual costs are settled upon completion, with unused reserved amounts automatically refunded to the organization wallet.

Top-Ups

Wallet credits are registered through platform administration following confirmed payment processing.

Section 3

Acceptable Use & Compliance

Marketing Suppression

Users must strictly respect customer opt-outs. Any inbound STOP keyword or manual opt-out will automatically populate the Marketing Suppression List, and messages to these numbers will be skipped.

Prohibited Content

Users are strictly prohibited from sending spam, fraudulent content, hate speech, or violating Meta’s messaging guidelines. Failure to comply may lead to immediate suspension.

Section 4

Limitation of Liability & Uptime

System Health

MsgOrbit strives for high availability, monitored via real-time health checks covering database and Redis connectivity. However, the platform relies on Meta's WhatsApp Cloud API infrastructure.
