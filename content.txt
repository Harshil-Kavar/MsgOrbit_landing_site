Eyebrow / Badge: 🔒 Data Privacy & Protection

Headline: Privacy Policy

Subheadline: At MsgOrbit, we take your data security and privacy seriously. This policy outlines how we collect, use, and protect your information across our multi-tenant SaaS platform.

Last Updated: September 2026

Section 1: Information We Collect
Organization & Account Details: Information provided during registration and profile management (Name, business email, phone number, address, website, GST number, and billing currency/timezone).

WhatsApp Business Data: Connected WhatsApp Business Account (WABA) metadata, phone numbers, verified display names, and approved message templates synced via Meta's APIs.

Contact Lists & Campaign Data: Customer contact information uploaded via CSV/Excel imports, custom tags, contact groups, and message broadcast history.

Usage & Device Metadata: Session metadata captured on every login (IP address, device type, browser, operating system) and immutable audit logs recording user actions.

Section 2: How We Use Your Information
Platform Delivery: To operate and maintain the multi-tenant SaaS dashboard, including campaign scheduling, bot execution, and conversation inbox synchronization.

Meta & API Compliance: To submit message templates for approval, process WhatsApp Flows, and enforce suppression lists (such as STOP keyword opt-outs).

Security & Authentication: To manage secure token rotation (JWT), hash passwords using bcrypt, and prevent unauthorized access or account enumeration.

Financial Settlements: To calculate pre-campaign estimated costs, reserve wallet funds, and process transaction histories.

Section 3: Tenant Isolation & Data Security
Strict Logical Separation: MsgOrbit implements robust tenant isolation architecture ensuring organizations can never access, view, or modify each other's data, contacts, or campaigns.

Encryption & Redaction: All sensitive fields (tokens, passwords, OTP codes hashed with HMAC-SHA256) are securely encrypted. Sensitive parameters are automatically redacted from system logs.

Section 4: User Rights & Contact
Data Access & Management: Organizations can view, edit, or delete their profile data, contact directories, and campaign settings at any time through their user panel.

Privacy Inquiries: For questions regarding this policy or data practices, contact our compliance team at privacy@msgorbit.com.

Headline: Terms of Service

Subheadline: Please read these terms carefully before using MsgOrbit. By accessing or using our platform, you agree to be bound by these conditions.

Last Updated: September 2026

Section 1: Acceptance of Terms & Platform Access
Service Scope: MsgOrbit provides a multi-tenant WhatsApp marketing, automation, and conversational inbox SaaS solution.

Account Eligibility: Users must represent a legitimate business entity and maintain active, verified WhatsApp Business Accounts (WABA) compliant with Meta's Commerce and Business Policies.

Organization Deactivation: System administrators reserve the right to suspend or deactivate organizations and associated user accounts that violate platform policies or terms of service.

Section 2: Prepaid Wallet & Financial Terms
Wallet Balance Model: Platform services operate on a prepaid wallet balance. Users must maintain sufficient funds to cover broadcast campaigns.

Cost Estimation & Settlement: Estimated message costs are calculated and reserved prior to campaign launch. Actual costs are settled upon completion, with unused reserved amounts automatically refunded to the organization wallet.

Top-Ups: Wallet credits are registered through platform administration following confirmed payment processing.

Section 3: Acceptable Use & Compliance
Marketing Suppression: Users must strictly respect customer opt-outs. Any inbound STOP keyword or manual opt-out will automatically populate the Marketing Suppression List, and messages to these numbers will be skipped.

Prohibited Content: Users are strictly prohibited from sending spam, fraudulent content, hate speech, or violating Meta’s messaging guidelines. Failure to comply may lead to immediate suspension.

Section 4: Limitation of Liability & Uptime
System Health: MsgOrbit strives for high availability, monitored via real-time health checks covering database and Redis connectivity. However, the platform relies on Meta's WhatsApp Cloud API infrastructure.

Limitation of Liability: MsgOrbit shall not be indirect, incidental, or consequential damages arising from campaign delivery failures, Meta policy changes, or third-party service interruptions.
