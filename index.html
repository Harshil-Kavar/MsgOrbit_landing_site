Got Questions?

Got Questions? We’ve Got Answers.

Learn how template clearance, tenant isolation, prepaid wallets, and automated compliance work on MsgOrbit.

You can create message templates directly inside the platform (categorized as Marketing, Utility, or Authentication with custom headers, buttons, and variables) and submit them to Meta for official approval with live preview bubbles.
Tenant isolation ensures that each organization operating on MsgOrbit has a completely secure, private sandbox. Organizations can never access each other’s contacts, campaigns, or wallet data.
MsgOrbit uses a prepaid wallet balance model. Before a campaign launches, the estimated cost is calculated and reserved from your wallet. Once completed, the actual cost is settled, and any unused reserved amount is automatically refunded.
MsgOrbit features an automated Marketing Suppression List. Inbound STOP keywords or user opt-outs automatically add the number to the suppression list, ensuring they are skipped in all future marketing campaigns to maintain compliance.
